Running an AI agent on your own infrastructure gives you control, but also responsibility. Here's how to do it safely.

## The Threat Model

Understanding what you're protecting against is the first step to good security.

## Key Principles

1. **Least Privilege**: Only grant the permissions your agent actually needs
2. **Input Validation**: Never trust user input blindly
3. **Audit Logging**: Keep records of what your agent does
