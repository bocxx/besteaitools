## ClawsHub 2.0 Has Arrived

We're thrilled to announce the release of ClawsHub 2.0, our most ambitious update ever.

## Key Features

### Multi-Modal Support
Process images, audio, and video alongside text for truly versatile AI assistance.

### 3x Faster Responses
Completely rewritten inference engine delivers blazing-fast results.

### 50+ New Integrations
Connect with Slack, Discord, Notion, Linear, and dozens more.

### Enhanced Privacy
All processing can now happen locally with our new on-device models.

```bash
claws upgrade --version 2.0
```
